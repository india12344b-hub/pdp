# PDP Homepage Structure

1. Hero
   - PDP logo + verification/check motif
   - Working tagline
   - Explore Talent / Find Opportunities
   - Create Your PDP — Free

2. Featured Candidate Showcase
   - Photo
   - Career-introduction video
   - Profession
   - Experience
   - Skills
   - Achievements
   - View PDP

3. Explore Talent
   - Industry filters
   - Featured professionals

4. Latest Opportunities
   - Industry/job cards
   - External application links initially

5. PDP Spotlight
   - Paid candidate showcase area (later)

6. Proof of Performance
   - Work videos
   - Projects/photos
   - Achievements
   - Certifications
   - Testimonials
   - Verified credentials

7. For Companies
   - Upload/paste/drop one or more openings
   - Local matching engine analyses each opening
   - Best candidate matches with reasons/evidence
   - Recruiter shortlist/contact workflow

8. Featured Companies

9. Candidate CTA

10. Footer
